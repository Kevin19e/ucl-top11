    How the numbers are made

    Each player's expected goals and expected assists per 90 minutes come from this season's league games plus last season's at half weight. Small samples are pulled toward the average for the position, so three hot games do not crown anyone.

    That rate is scaled by how many goals the team is expected to score in this match, multiplied by the minutes the player is likely to get, and topped up for penalty takers. A Poisson model turns the result into a chance of at least one goal or assist.

    A clean sheet chance comes from how many goals the opponent is expected to score, multiplied by the chance the player starts. For defenders the two chances are combined into one number. No attacking number is allowed above 75%.

    Each eleven is a 4-3-3: one keeper, two full-backs, two centre-backs, three midfielders and three forwards, with at most two defenders from one club. Wingers count as forwards and number tens as midfielders. The second XI is the best eleven left once the first is picked.

    The range is a rough plus or minus 20% on the underlying rates, wider for players with a role or selection doubt. These are statistical estimates, not guarantees or betting advice.
