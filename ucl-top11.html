    How the numbers are made

    1. Form. Each player's expected goals and expected assists per 90 minutes, from this season's league games plus last season's at half weight. Small samples are pulled toward the average for the position, so three hot games do not crown anyone.
    2. Opponent. That rate is scaled by how many goals his team is expected to score in this match.
    3. Minutes. Then multiplied by the minutes he is likely to play, with a top-up for penalty takers.
    4. Chance. A Poisson model turns the result into the chance of at least one goal or assist. A clean sheet chance comes from how many goals the opponent is expected to score. For defenders the two are combined.
    5. Team sheet. Each eleven is a 4-3-3: one keeper, two full-backs, two centre-backs, three midfielders and three forwards, with at most two defenders from one club. Wingers count as forwards and number tens as midfielders.

    The likely range is a rough plus or minus 20% on the underlying rates, wider for players with a role or selection doubt. No attacking number is allowed above 75%. These are statistical estimates, not guarantees or betting advice.
